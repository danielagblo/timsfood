Our story

Ghanaian roots. Home flavour.

Tims Food is the online shop for TIMS NMC jars packed in Accra. Weannimix, Wheatmix, Ricemix, millet and sorghum blends, Hausa Koko, corn grits and family sizes.

We keep the range simple: sealed factory jars, labels you can read, and shipping across Ghana. The mixes are for everyday porridge, koko and family cooking — the same pantry staples people already cook at home.
